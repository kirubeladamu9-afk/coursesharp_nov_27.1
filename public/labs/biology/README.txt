COURSESHARP LABS PACK

Structure: <subject>/<lab>/index.html (Coursesharp page) + sim.html (original simulation, unmodified).
labs.json lists every lab with title, subject, grade band and suggested STEM Lab topic.
Grade bands and topics are suggestions - edit labs.json to match your curriculum.

HOW TO USE
1. Copy the subject folders into your site's public/static folder, e.g. /public/labs/
2. Link to /labs/<subject>/<lab>/index.html from a lesson, or iframe it.
3. Keep the attribution line on every page (already included).
